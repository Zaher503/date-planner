- The current fall hours (with Wednesday and Thursday closed) are on the Contact page, but the app showed the general hours from another page, which wrongly say the park is open every weekday.
- No ticket prices at all, even though every tour has a "Starting at $..." price on the Zipline Tours page.
- The Zipline Tours page was never opened: it was not among the pages the importer picked.

## What changed
The importer now opens up to six extra pages from a site (was four), looks at hours, contact and price pages first, and also opens pages named like tours, packages and passes. It now also picks up price lines that the page reader used to drop (like "Starting at $99" on a price card), together with the lines naming the item. And it is told to prefer day-by-day hours for the current season over a "typical hours" line.

## What it shows AFTER (fixed code, not yet live; same link rerun on the home PC)
- Hours now include the fall day-by-day list with Wednesday and Thursday CLOSED, next to the typical hours. Better, but not fully right: the weekly-hours field still comes from the site's hidden data (Friday to Tuesday, 9 to 5), which has the wrong times.
- The Zipline Tours page is now read and the price cards reach the model.
- Prices are **still missing**: the model lists "Original Zipline Course" and "Advanced Zipline Courses" but leaves the price empty. One experiment made it invent a $0 "Spectator" ticket, so I backed that wording out.

## Still not fixed
- Prices on "Starting at" price cards are not filled in. Suggestion: read the cards directly (item name plus the number after "Starting at") instead of asking the model; new `IM-27`.
- When a site's hidden data and its page text disagree on hours, the hidden data still wins for the weekly-hours field. Suggestion: prefer the page's day-by-day hours when they list closed days; new `IM-28`.
- Sky Gym and rock wall prices and durations (`IM-07` area) are not read.

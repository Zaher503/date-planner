## 2. What happened
The live import tester read the video and its description. Then it stopped at "reading with the local model" after 7 seconds: the AI hub answered with an error (502).

The cause is on the Zephyrus itself, not in the app. The graphics card's driver has stopped responding. There are 554 copies of the graphics-card status tool waiting on it and none of them can finish. The load average is about 563 and only about 200 MB of memory is free. Even listing the Docker containers hangs. Until that is cleared, **any import on the live app that needs the model fails the same way**, and Foodbox and the transcriber are probably down too.

I did not restart anything. A reboot of the Zephyrus (or a driver reset) is needed. It is at the top of the roadmap as `LC-11`.

# 2026-10-17

**Tested:** https://www.thehollywoodroosevelt.com/events/los-angeles/down-the-rabbit-hole (from bookmarks.txt, event listing page, no JSON-LD, local LLM extraction).

## Extracted vs verified

The page is a calendar of four recurring events at The Hollywood Roosevelt (7000 Hollywood Blvd, LA 90028, +1 323-785-7000): Classic Cinema Night, Down the Rabbit Hole (every other Saturday 8 PM, 18+, Cinegrill Theater), Magic at the Library Bar (Sundays 6 PM), Chess and Jazz (first Monday, Spare Room). Each has its own Eventbrite link, except Chess and Jazz.

The pipeline found all four, but gave every one the same price ($32.64-$74.84) and the same ticket URL (Classic Cinema Night's), so "Down the Rabbit Hole" showed the wrong price and the wrong ticket link.

## Changed

- `pricing.enrich`: on a listing of several events, an event with no ticket URL now takes the page link whose address carries its own title (`own_ticket_link`), and is skipped if none matches. Before, each one walked the page's first ticket link. Test added in `test_pricing.py`. After the fix a live rerun gave Down the Rabbit Hole $55.20-$139.49 with its own link; Classic Cinema Night and Magic at the Library Bar also got their own links, and Chess and Jazz got no price (no link).

## Not changed (flagged, not fixed)

- Venue address, phone and the real venue name are missing (the name comes out as "Roosevelt" / "Cinegrill Theater"; the address sits in the page footer). Fix idea: pass the footer address/site name to the LLM as a page-level hint, or let venue resolution search "The Hollywood Roosevelt". The SearXNG tunnel was down in this run, so venue lookup could not help.

- Chess and Jazz has no `event_title` (the LLM put it in the name only), and Magic at the Library Bar came back with a price of 0-0 that I did not verify against its ticket page.

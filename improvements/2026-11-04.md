- `venue.footer_text` now returns nothing when the footer matches "City/County/Town of ...", "City Hall" or civic CMS names (not "City of Industry"/"Commerce"); both the single-page and venue-enrichment paths use it.
- Cause: the footer was passed to the extractor as the site's own address ("apply to every event"), and the prompt told the model to use it. Test: `test_city_site_footer_is_not_taken_as_the_park_address`.
- The page itself returned 403 to plain fetches from this PC (Cloudflare); the live pipeline read it via its own route.

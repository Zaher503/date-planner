# 2026-10-25: Paramount Drive-In Theatres (Now Playing page)

**Link tested:** https://www.paramountdrivein.com/now-playing/ (from bookmarks). The "Now Playing" page of a small drive-in movie theater in Paramount. Tested on the live import service (the same one the app uses; nothing was saved).

*Screenshots: not taken this run (the browser capture tool was not used on this unattended run). The raw output from the live service is at the bottom; the before/after table below is the comparison.*

## What the live app showed BEFORE

| Field | Real value (checked on the site) | Live import | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Paramount Drive-In Theatres | Paramount Drive In Theatres | correct |
| Street address | 7770 Rosecrans Avenue, Paramount, CA 90723 | empty | **missed** |
| Phone | 562.630.SHOW (7469) | 562.630.7469 | correct |
| Price | $14 adults, $7 kids 3-11, toddlers free | tiers right, but the item was marked **Free** | **wrong** |
| Price range | $7 to $14 for anyone paying | $0 to $14 | partly wrong (the $0 is the toddler line) |
| Show times | 7:30 pm and 10:40 pm, one ticket per show | not shown | missed (not a clear fix, see below) |
| Box office | opens one hour before the movie | not shown | missed |
| Map pin | in Paramount | correct (found from the site's map) | correct |
| Photos | movie posters | 3 posters | correct |

- The street address was printed only in the small footer at the bottom of the site, and the part of the importer that cleans the page for the model throws footers away. So the model never saw it.
- The place was marked "Free" because the price list says "Toddlers (2 and under) Free". A $14 ticket place showed "Free" on the card.
- Which movie is playing on which night is not on the page as text (posters only), so no movie list could come out.

## What changed

The importer now also reads the footer of a venue's site (where small venues keep their address and phone), and a place with real ticket prices is no longer marked "Free" just because one line (toddlers) is free.

## What it shows AFTER (fixed code, not yet live)

Same link, rerun on the fixed code on this PC:
- Street address: **7770 Rosecrans Avenue Paramount, CA 90723** (was empty).
- No longer marked Free; the card shows its prices instead.
- Phone, photos and prices tiers unchanged and still right.

## Still not fixed

- The price range still starts at $0 because of the free-toddler line, so the card reads "$0-14" style ranges. Suggestion: ignore a $0 tier when other tiers are paid (small, but it is a decision about how free kids tickets show).
- Show times and "box office opens an hour before" are not picked up. Suggestion: the existing item `IM-04` (times in event text) covers this partly; a "daily showtimes" idea for drive-ins is bigger.
- The importer treats the page as a place, which is right here.

- Cause 1: `parse_page` uses trafilatura, which drops `<footer>`; the venue lookup read only that text. Fix: `venue.footer_text()` appends the footer text (max 800 chars) to each venue page before `read_facts`. Test: `test_footer_text_keeps_address_the_article_extractor_drops`.
- Cause 2: `llm/extract.py::to_hint` kept `is_free` whenever the word "free" appeared in the text; `pricing.read_price` had the same weakness. Both now clear `is_free` when `price_max > 0`. Tests: `test_free_toddlers_line_does_not_make_a_paid_place_free`, `test_free_toddlers_do_not_make_the_venue_free`.

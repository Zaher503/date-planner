## 1. Link tested
https://www.sunbursttrain.com/ (from the bookmarks): the home page of a railbike business that runs pedal-and-electric rail bikes through citrus groves from the Santa Paula depot. Tested on the live import service (nothing was saved).

## 2. What the live app showed BEFORE
No screenshots today (the desktop screenshot tools were not set up in this run); the raw result is at the bottom and in `raw/2026-11-06.json`.

- Two sale banners would have shown up in the review list as events.
- The business itself (what you actually book) never became a place.
- Price and times sit in a booking widget the page reader cannot see.

## 3. What changed
Sale banners ("Save 15%...", a discount window described as "Save 35%...") are now kept as notes on the venue ("Offers: ...") instead of becoming events. The import tester also now runs the same step as the real app (it was skipping it, so it showed different results from the app for home pages).

## 4. What it shows AFTER
Checked by replaying the saved result through the new rule (not a live rerun; the fix is not live until merged):
- Toy Drive Train stays a dated event.
- The 35% discount window is no longer an event.
- The 15% weekday banner no longer an event; it becomes a note on the place "The Sunburst" ("Offers: Fall into Savings! Save 15% ...").

## 5. Still not fixed
- The ride itself is not saved as the place's main experience. Suggestion: ask the model for the main experience when the page is full of seasonal banners (`IM-32`).
- Price per bike and trip times are inside a booking widget. Suggestion: try the browser reader on those embeds, never guess a number (`IM-31`).
- The note on the place ends up as the only description; with `IM-32` it would read better.
